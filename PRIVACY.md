# Privacy Policy

YouTube Watch Later Sorter does not collect, store, sell, transmit, or share personal data.

The extension runs locally in your browser and only interacts with YouTube pages that match `https://www.youtube.com/*`.

It uses browser extension permissions to:

- read the currently loaded Watch Later playlist rows,
- click YouTube playlist menu actions on your behalf,
- inject the content script into an already open YouTube tab after the extension has been reloaded.

There is no backend server, telemetry, analytics, or third-party data transfer.
